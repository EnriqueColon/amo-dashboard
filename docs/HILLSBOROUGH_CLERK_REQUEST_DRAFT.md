# Draft request to the Hillsborough County Clerk — foreclosure complaints

**Status:** DRAFT for the owner to edit and send. **Nothing has been sent.** Written 3 Nov 2026.
**Fill in:** every `[bracket]`. Send from a company address so the Clerk can reply to a named person.
**Where to send:** the Clerk's records / public-records contact (confirm the current address and the "Request Copies of Court Documents" form on `hillsclerk.com`; the form may be the required channel for the first request).

---

## Why this asks for the complaint, not the mortgage

The mortgage recorded in Official Records gives the property address and the original loan amount, but **not the unpaid balance.** The unpaid principal appears only in the **complaint** (the paragraph that says what the borrower still owes on the note) and in the note and exhibits filed with it. The $500,000 test depends on that number, so the request is for the complaint **with all its exhibits**.

## Draft message

**Subject:** Request for foreclosure complaints (Circuit Civil) and question on automated access

Dear Records Department,

I am [Name], [Title] at [Company], a [one line: e.g. real estate credit investor] in [City]. We review newly filed mortgage foreclosures in Hillsborough County to identify commercial and non-homestead loans in distress.

**1. What we are asking for**

The initial **Complaint, with every exhibit filed with it** (the promissory note, the mortgage, any guaranty, assignments and the payoff or default letters), for Circuit Civil cases of these types, filed on or after [start date]:

- Mortgage Foreclosure – Commercial (all amount bands)
- Mortgage Foreclosure – NonHomestead (all amount bands; we are most interested in "$250,000 or More")

We understand that confidential and exempt information is redacted under the Florida Standards for Access to Electronic Court Records, and we do not ask for anything that is not publicly viewable.

**2. How we would like to receive it**

In order of preference:

1. A **standing electronic delivery** (secure download or SFTP) of the day's new Complaints for those case types, as PDF, once a day or once a week.
2. A **one-time bulk copy** of the complaints for cases filed from [start date] to date, followed by a periodic update.
3. If neither is possible: written confirmation of how we may obtain these documents in volume.

**3. Fees**

Please tell us the cost of each option, and whether any of it can be provided without charge. We understand the Clerk's per-document fees for certified copies; we do not need certified copies, only copies of the documents as filed.

**4. A question about the online portal (HOVER)**

HOVER shows these documents, but it is protected against automated tools, and we do not want to work around that. May we be given **permission, an API, or an approved method** for retrieving non-confidential documents from HOVER in volume? If the answer is that we should use the Clerk's daily and monthly public data files plus manual requests, please say so and we will do that.

**5. How we use the information**

We read the complaints for the unpaid principal balance, the property address and the loan terms. We do not republish the documents. [Edit this sentence to match how the owner wants to describe the use; keep it accurate.]

**6. Contact**

[Name] · [Title] · [Company] · [phone] · [email] · [mailing address]

Please let us know if a different form or office is the right channel for this request, or if a written request on company letterhead is preferred.

Thank you,
[Name]

## Notes for the owner before sending

- **Legal wording:** I avoided citing statutes. Court records are governed by Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.420 and the Access Standards the portal itself cites; the Clerk's counsel may prefer the request framed that way. Add a citation only if you want to; have a lawyer check it first.
- **"Our use" paragraph:** keep it true. If the Clerk's terms forbid commercial reuse of the images, this is where it would come up.
- **Redaction time:** the Clerk's Circuit Civil page says paper or electronic copies can take about 4 business days for redaction processing. A standing feed may be slower than the 24-hour pace of the daily case list.
- **Fallback if they decline:** a person downloads the short list from HOVER in a normal browser (about 46 candidate cases a month), and the pipeline reads them from a folder.
- **What the Clerk's public files already give us without any request:** the daily list of new cases (case type, parties, addresses, attorney) and the monthly docket-event files. They do **not** contain the amount owed.
